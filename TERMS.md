# Terms of Use - Backup Power Finder

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Backup Power Finder provides product comparison and shopping guidance. It does not sell products, process payments, guarantee availability, or guarantee that a product will operate a specific device.

Runtime calculations are estimates based on stated or modeled load and usable battery capacity. Starting wattage, temperature, battery age, inverter losses, settings, and real-world usage can change results. Verify current price, specifications, compatibility, warranty, returns, and delivery on the merchant website before purchase.

The app recommends battery power stations for indoor use and does not recommend running fuel-burning generators indoors. Follow manufacturer instructions and applicable electrical and fire-safety guidance. For CPAP or other medical equipment, confirm requirements with the device manufacturer or a qualified clinician before relying on a backup system.

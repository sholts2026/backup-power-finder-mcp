# Privacy Policy - Backup Power Finder

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Backup Power Finder is a ChatGPT shopping assistant for portable battery power stations and solar generator bundles.

## Data categories and purposes

The app processes shopping criteria a user chooses to provide, including budget, use case, devices, estimated wattage, target runtime, solar preference, and portability preference. Those details are used in memory to calculate and explain product recommendations.

App-level click records contain time, app, merchant, product SKU, result rank, broad intent tags, destination, and a random click reference. They are used for affiliate attribution, aggregate performance measurement, debugging, and abuse prevention. Conversation text is not written to click records.

The app does not ask for payment details, passwords, government identifiers, precise home addresses, medical records, or account identifiers.

## Recipients

Request criteria are processed by OpenAI and our hosting provider as needed to operate the app. When a user voluntarily opens an offer, the merchant and approved affiliate network receive the click URL and attribution reference under their own privacy policies. We do not sell personal data.

## Retention and controls

Product recommendation inputs are not stored by this application after the request completes. App-level click records are retained for no more than 30 days. Hosting providers may retain operational and security logs under their published policies.

Opening merchant links is optional. Users may avoid entering identifying details and may contact us to request access, deletion, or help with a privacy concern. Random click references are not tied to a ChatGPT account, so we may be unable to associate a record with a specific person.
